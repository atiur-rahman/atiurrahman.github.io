## Projects

### Autonomous car

A four-wheel autonomous car driven by an ESP32. Ultrasonic sensors detect obstacles, an L298N driver runs the motors, and a buck converter steps down power from two 18650 Li-ion cells.

### Line follower robot (LFR)

A line follower robot built on an Arduino Nano. A curved sensor array reads the black line on the track and the robot steers itself along it.
